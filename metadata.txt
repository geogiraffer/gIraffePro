    gIraffePro supports early environmental screening for linear infrastructure projects. Its preparation tool assigns ratings from 1 to 3 to protected-area and natural-feature datasets using a suggested weighting scheme that expert users can adjust. The processing tool filters these datasets within configurable distances of a project axis and presents the results using traffic-light ratings. Datasets can be selected individually or in combination; at least one is required. The results help project managers and steering groups consider potential environmental constraints before planning options become fixed. They provide preliminary guidance and do not replace a detailed environmental assessment. The source code is licensed under GPL-3.0-or-later.
